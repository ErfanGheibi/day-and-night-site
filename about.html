About Day&Night

Hi, I'm Erfan, a student from the School of Computer Science and Engineering at The University of New South Wales.

Why would you want to know sunrise and sunset times: - Avoid walking home in the dark for safety. - Sleep and circadian rythm research and planning. - Planninig outdoor activites such as: gardening, jogging, photography. - Religious/cultural practices, determine times for certain observances. - And many other applications.
